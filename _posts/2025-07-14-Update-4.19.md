## Sea of Souls VIII 
* New Class: Augurs!
	* A fresh take on a previously obscure Heartverse class, Augurs represent the archetype of a being created for a grander purpose. Drawing from characters such as Persona 3's Ryoji Mochizuki and Devil Survivor 2's Al Saiduq, Augurs are rewarded for learning spells of their 'Signature Type', while still being able to dish out serious damage with access to 4 Types and unique, powerful Feats.

## Sea of Souls VI - Bloom Rework! 
* As one of our most unique subtypes, Bloom has struggled to dig a reliable niche in the Grimoire metagame. Despite being a very powerful support and control option that is paired with one of the most reliable damage Types, its high complexity and scattered effects made Bloom a very difficult option to build for. These changes aim to make Bloom a bit more approachable and pack a much heavier punch. 

* Corrected several instances of "Blossom" and "Bloom" being used interchangeably in Spell categories 

### New Content 
* New Feat: _Dance of Dying Leaves_. Essentially, Gigantic Breath for Pollen effects. Should make it much easier to proc Lose Thy Way, Dahlias, and Crimson Roses 
	* Your Active Persona must have the Wind Type to activate this Feat
	* Spells you cast that affect units within Pollen spaces can now target units adjacent to Pollen spaces. If a spell already affects units in Pollen-adjacent spaces, it can now affect units within 2 meters of the Pollen space. Other targeting limitations still apply. 
	* Can be chosen up to two times. 
	
* New Spell: Primrose and Perfume. This is meant to be a splashable option for support-oriented Blooom Users. Although it has hard Repress and "once-per-turn" caps, being a Free action means you can setup Pollen then trigger Primrose to both support your allies and advance your own plays. Note that, RAW, it can be cast outside of your turn through Petals for Armor and its effects will stick during your turn. 
	* Tier I [Bloom, Repress, Wind (A)], Caster, Free, Until the end of your turn 
	* Repress and spend 1 Energy to cast. When you use a non-Special Consumable item targeting only yourself, one User in a Pollen space can spend their Interrupt action and RP equal to the that item’s listed cost: That User also gains the effect of that consumable. The party can only cast this spell once per round.

* New Feat: For Evergreen. A consistency booster for damage-focused Bloom users. Allows Users to recycle and preserve resources for longer fights, and assists with trapping and bursting down tougher targets. 
	* Whenever Pollen under your control would be removed by duration, or used as part of casting a Bloom Category spell, you gain one Evergreen Counter for each affected Pollen. During your turn, for no action cost, once per round, you can choose to remove a certain amount of Evergreen Counters (choose 1 option): 
		*2X: You gain +X Movement Allowance this turn
		*3X: Place X Pollen in spaces you can see 
		*5: Your next hit roll gains +1 HDC
	* Requires CLV. 4 or higher and the Wind Type to activate 
	* Can only be chosen once 


### Updated Content 
* Their Garden in Full Bloom: Effect reworked. This is a small but weighty change to boost the viability of mixed Wind-Bloom builds and some niche Elemental Basic Attack builds
	* _Once per round, when you deal Wind damage to at least one unit, and do not place Pollen as part of the same action, you can place Pollen in one space you can see as part of the damage-dealing action. If this damage is Critical_, you can, _instead of the above_, spend X Energy to place Pollen on the spaces of up to X _+1_ target(s) who took damage from that spell

* Path of Yellow Carnations: Effect updated. Forced movement is a small niche that is never truly centralized as a build option, so giving that mechanic a bit more love is an inexpensive buff that will hopefully make Bloom more universally interesting to players
	* Until the end of each affected unit's turn, they take Wind damage equal to their SKL for each space they move ~~as their movement action~~ _from their current position. This includes forcing movement through spells or attacks_.

* March Forth, Edelweiss: Effect updated. As a T5 Default action that incentivizes a build path with several different Types, Edelweiss struggled to be a viable option on most party compositions. This buff aims to make it easier to splash into parties with squishier party members and gives it better synergy with Petals for Armor
	* The next time you _would_ take damage from a spell of the chosen Type, you gain <Type> Affinity +1 ~~for that spell~~ _until the end of the current turn_. 
	
* Maken's Host: Now gain two extra Combat Skill points at character generation
	* Maken Hosts exist in a delicate balancing position where they are powerful but unpopular, due to the obscurity of their source material and the complexity of building them. This small change is aimed at rounding up their hyper-aggressive play style, while keeping their power scaling at a reasonable level with other high-complexity classes	

## Sea of Souls VII 
* Maken Shao: General buffs. Similar to their Host counterparts, Shao are difficult to build and require deep knowledge of the Grimoire to truly master, so a few tweaks became necessary to make them more welcoming. 
	* Summer Rain: Gains one extra Combat Skill point and gain access to either a free Feat or a Trigram Cut 
	* Spring Thunder: Sacrificing a higher-Tier spell now grants access to lower-Tier Conditions
		* The T3 and T5 Conditions have been swapped 
	* Autumn Leaves: 
		* Maneuver Points are now gained every 3 levels, rather than every 5 levels
